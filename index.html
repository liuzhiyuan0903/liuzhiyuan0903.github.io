Zhiyuan Liu (刘之源) is a Ph.D. student at the University of Connecticut. Her research interests revolve around Business Analytics, Social Media Analytics, Multimodal Data Analysis, and Artificial Intelligence. She employs techniques such as Data Mining, Natural Language Processing, Machine/Deep Learning, and Econometrics/Statistical Analysis to explore these areas.

She received her MSc in Management Science and Engineering and BSc in Computer Science and Technology from Renmin University of China.
